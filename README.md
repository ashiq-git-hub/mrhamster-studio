## 📐 Tactile Retro Design System

1. **Credentials Gate (Restricted Access)**:
   - Full retro lock screen protecting the studio.
   - Enter `ashiq` / `ashiq14726` to unlock.
   - Added a **`🔒 Lock`** button in the top navigation to lock access anytime.

2. **Warm Vintage Stone Palette (Not Washed-Out White)**:
   - Interface background is a rich warm vintage stone/clay tone (`#eae4d8` and `#dfd8cb`) rather than flat clinical white.
   - Contrast between cards (`#ffffff`) and the background gives every section crisp definition.

3. **Distinct Shadows for Each Block**:
   - Each control card is elevated with a tactile dual drop shadow (`0 4px 18px rgba(28, 24, 18, 0.09)` + `3px 3px 0px rgba(200, 192, 178, 0.5)`).
   - Buttons, ratio tiles, and input fields all feature physical depth and tactile hover feedback.

4. **High-Contrast Brand Logo & Avatar Visibility**:
   - **Header Mascot Logo Box**: Styled with a solid dark vintage ink background (`#1c1b18`) and 3D shadow so the mascot logo stands out instantly.
   - **Profile Avatar Frame**: Recessed tone (`#ded7c9`) with an inset border and shadow so the white Mog Hamster doodle pops out clearly.

5. **Defined Avatar Frame (Circle Ring & Square Box Rim)**:
   - Both **Circle** and **Square** shapes in the generated post feature a crisp, defined frame that smoothly bridges the pitch-black post (`#000000`) and the white avatar (`#ffffff`).
   - Default color: **Slate Titanium (`#4a4f5c`)** — a sophisticated neutral rim that harmonizes the white avatar with the black canvas.
   - Options include: *Slate Titanium*, *Deep Charcoal*, *Vintage Brass*, *Warm Ivory*, *Classic Platinum*, and *None*.

6. **Authentic Dark Instagram Post (The Canvas)**:
   - **The Post remains 100% pitch-black OLED (`#000000`)**: High-contrast white text (`#f7f9f9`), gray handle (`#71767b`), blue verified badge (`#1d9bf0`), and sharp meme framing.
   - Sits on an exhibition pedestal with a deep drop shadow in the center of the workspace.

7. **Instagram Aspect Ratio Boxes**:
   - **`4:5 Portrait`** (`1080 × 1350`) — Instagram Feed Standard
   - **`1:1 Square`** (`1080 × 1080`) — Classic Feed
   - **`9:16 Story / Reel`** (`1080 × 1920`) — Fullscreen Vertical
   - **`16:9 Landscape`** (`1920 × 1080`) — Banner / Cover

8. **Lossless High-Quality Export**:
   - Native 1080p resolution, crystal-clear 24-bit PNG export (`canvas.toDataURL('image/png', 1.0)`).
   - Zero compression grain, artifacts, or blur.
   - One-click **`Copy to Clipboard`** and **`↺ Defaults`**.
